## en-US

**Name (30)**: Forklift & Tow Exam Grader

**Subtitle (30)**: Korean licence practical tests

**Keywords (100)**: forklift,licence,license,korea,practical,test,trailer,tow,towing,wrecker,grader,exam,course,qnet

**Promotional text**:
When you fail a practical test it happens in a second. This app stops on that second and shows what was measured.

**Description**:
Korean forklift operator candidates rarely fail on the big things. They fail because the forks were at 52 cm in the travel section, or the pallet landed 21 cm off the reference pallet, or the front wheels crossed the start line 61 seconds after the whistle. This app grades exactly those limits.

The forklift course is built from the drawing in Q-Net's public exam "Course driving and handling" (posted 22 December 2025): the ring with its centre cross, the drum in work zone 1, the reference pallet and back line in work zone 2, the start/finish line and the parking guide line. The clock runs from the front wheels crossing the start line to crossing the finish line, as in the exam.

The app measures 16 of the exam's 18 disqualification rules. When one is broken the run freezes and shows the value against the limit, for example "fork height 54 cm > 50 cm", with the rule number and a measurement line in the scene. A slider rewinds the last seconds so you can see where it went wrong.

The rulebook lists every rule with its source and marks the ones the app checks. Tap a rule and a drill starts just before that mistake. Deductions are not published by the exam, so the app's deductions are shown as estimates.

Exam-day mode follows the real procedure: the examiner's whistle, the 1-minute start window and a hard stop at 4:00. The result is a score sheet split into loading (45) and unloading (55), pass at 60.

The Class 1 special towing licence yard test (large trailer, small trailer, wrecker) is included too, graded with Annex 24 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules (revised 24 February 2026): line touches, time over, the 20-second start, the seatbelt, pass at 90, with the same freeze frame, rulebook and score sheet.

No login, no ads, no purchases, no data collection. Korean and English.

Independent app, not affiliated with HRDK, the National Police Agency or the Road Traffic Authority. A real machine handles differently; check each agency's current notice for official rules.

**What's new (1.0)**: Forklift course rebuilt from the Q-Net public exam drawing; freeze frame with measurements, rulebook, exam-day mode, score sheet and English.

## Review notes (English)

Hello, this is a practice grader for two Korean licence practical tests, with no login, no purchases and no network use.

60-second path:
1. Launch. The home screen has two exam cards. To read it in English, tap "한국어 / English" in the bottom row (the app reloads in English; it also follows the device language on first launch).
2. Forklift card → "Rulebook". The 18 disqualification rules of the official Q-Net public exam are listed with their source; 16 are marked "Checked by the app".
3. Tap "Drill →" on row (14) "Forks more than 50 cm above the ground in the travel section". The forklift starts in the travel section with the forks at 42 cm. Push the Lift lever (right side, drag it towards "Up") or press R on a keyboard.
4. At 50 cm the run freezes: "Forks above 50 cm in the travel section · fork height 51 cm > 50 cm · Q-Net public exam, item (14)", with a red measurement line and a rewind slider. Tap "Score sheet" to see the graded sheet.
5. Back on home, the second card "Class 1 special: large trailer" → "Open the towing yard" opens the towing test (Annex 24 rulebook, mock exams, score sheet).

The separate towing app (6823617013) was merged into this one and will not be released on its own.
